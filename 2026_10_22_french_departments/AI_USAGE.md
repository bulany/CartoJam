# AI Usage & Provenance Disclaimer

This CartoJam experiment (**The Human Topography of France**) was created with extensive AI assistance as part of a weekly rapid-prototyping cartography jam.

* **Concept, Direction & Voice Spec:** Bulany
* **Architecture & Workflow Engineering:** Gemini Argon
* **Application Code & Data Bundling:** Google AI Studio App Builder (`Gemini 3.8 Flash` in Max Mode)

### Machine-Readable Session & Token Log
Full parallel session timestamps, human voice-recording durations, model compute durations, and token counts are stored in [`ai_log.json`](./ai_log.json) and rendered in the interactive **Provenance Waterfall & Heatmap** on the main CartoJam portfolio page.
