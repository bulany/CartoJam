What does WOPR stand for?
WOPR stands for War Operation Plan Response (pronounced "whopper"), the military artificial intelligence and tactical supercomputer from the classic 1983 film WarGames, programmed by Dr. Stephen Falken at NORAD Cheyenne Mountain.

Dual-Color Solar Terminator: The simulation calculates the exact subsolar point from the Day of the Year and UTC Hour (using Spencer's solar declination formula). Hemispheres facing the sun are rendered in Phosphor Emerald Green, while the night hemisphere across the twilight terminator shifts into NORAD Alert Crimson Red.

Flight Trajectory Mechanics: Great Circle paths are calculated using spherical slerp interpolation with parabolic cruise altitude. Layovers feature radar station holds while the Earth continues to rotate beneath.

Architecture: Built with pure offline-first vanilla JavaScript, WebGL (Three.js), and zero-dependency local vector geometries.
